Freemium

Native rust

The entire program is written with native rust and is ran on your machine locally, nothing gets sent to any of our servers and you don't have to worry about us invading your privacy, with the IDE being written in rust also means it is extremely performant, we seek to only create quality and friendly products.

Non-invasive data collecting

We refuse to invade your privacy for money and other benefits, we do not collect any personal data, the only data we will ever collect are your optional reviews and preferences to make the IDE even better.
